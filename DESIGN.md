# FORNIX Design Direction

Source: PRD §6.3–6.4 (product owner direction, not agent invention).

## Identity

Personal R&D workspace for engineers. Technical, analytical, minimal. Hybrid of Notion (content), Linear (progress), Obsidian (knowledge), GitBook (reading docs).

## Personality

Technical · Engineering-oriented · Analytical · Experimental · Professional · Minimalist · Future-ready

## Theme

- Default: light
- Optional: dark
- Not a dense admin dashboard

## Palette

- Neutrals: near-white surfaces, slate text
- Accent: indigo / blue (one accent family)
- Max 2–3 core + 1 accent

## Typography

- UI: Geist Sans (PRD allows Inter or Geist; Geist chosen for sharper technical tone)
- Code: system monospace stack until editor phase

## Icons

Lucide (`@lucide/angular`)

## Dials

ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

Calm entry, slightly varied section rhythm inside the app shell, motion limited to state transitions and focus.
